# TTRSolver Logo Candidates

Minimalist logo designs for **TTRSolver**, synthesizing Toontown's iconic Throw gags with solver decision tree geometry.

---

## Spotlight: The Toontown Cream Pie Decision Tree

Inspired by Candidate 1, styled authentically after Toontown's **Cream Pie Slice (Level 3 Throw)** with fluffy white whipped cream filling, a golden baked fluted crust, and a whipped cream swirl dollop. From the cream rises a clean, geometric decision tree with binary simulation nodes in **Sound Blue (#3A86FF)** and **Throw Orange (#F77F00)**.
